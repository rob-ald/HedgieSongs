Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

kidshorts is a private application used only by the owner of the Hedgie Songs YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's consent, the app uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos, manage playlists and read the channel's own analytics. It accesses only the channel owner's account.

What the app does not do

The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any viewer or third party. It does not use cookies or tracking. Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time from the Google account security settings.

Third-party terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
